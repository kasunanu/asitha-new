BREATH * SOUL * MELODY * HARMONY *

Blending centuries-old bansuri traditions with modern acoustic arrangements to craft unforgettable musical journeys across the globe.

"Music begins where the words end, carried by the pure breath of the bamboo flute."

Asitha Senevirathne is an acclaimed Sri Lankan instrumentalist and bansuri maestro, celebrated for weaving ancient classical ragas with evocative cinematic sensibilities. Through years of mastery over Eastern bamboo flutes, Western classical woodwinds, and modern acoustic arrangements, his sound has resonated across leading concert stages, critically acclaimed studio productions, and high-profile film scores. Every performance is an intimate dialogue between breath and spirit—whether harmonizing with full orchestras, elevating sacred wedding vows, or headlining contemporary fusion concerts worldwide.
